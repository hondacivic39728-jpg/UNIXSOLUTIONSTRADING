About Us

Company Overview

Unix Solutions Trading LLC is a leading supplier and distributor of signage, car tinting, and MEP materials. Founded in 2025 as an advertising and event management company, Unix Solutions established a highly reputable client base before strategically diversifying into the trading and distribution of high-quality signage and commercial materials.

Unix Solutions is your One-Stop Source for Premium Signage, Car Tinting, MEP Materials and other Items. We supply top-tier signage materials, high-performance window tinting films, and robust MEP components to contractors, businesses, and fabricators who refuse to compromise on quality.

At Unix Solutions we bridge the gap between global manufacturers and local contractors. We understand that your projects rely on the integrity of your materials. That is why we source only certified, durable, and high-performance products designed to meet strict industry standards.

Whether you are wrapping a fleet of vehicles, fabricating building facades, or installing complex mechanical systems, we deliver the materials you need—on time, every time.

To supply certified, durable, and high-performance materials that power projects across signage, automotive, and MEP markets.
